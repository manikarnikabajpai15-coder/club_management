# Club Management Website

A modern **Club Management Website** designed to simplify the process of managing college clubs, events, members, and activities in one place.

## 🚀 Features

* 👥 **Club Management** – Create, manage, and explore different college clubs.
* 📅 **Event Management** – Organize and display upcoming and past club events.
* 🙋 **Member Management** – Manage club members and their participation.
* 🔐 **User Authentication** – Secure login and registration functionality.
* 📢 **Announcements** – Share important updates and club notifications.
* 📊 **Dashboard** – Get an overview of clubs, members, and events.
* 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
